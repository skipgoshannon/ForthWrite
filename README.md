This is just a simple little text editor styled to look like an old computer terminal from the 80s or late 90s. I'm just making it for myself, in part to learn how to do it with the help of claude, and in part because I want a writing app that does all the things I want. I've added quite a lot of journaling and story writing prompts for when I have some writers block. It's not anything exciting or new, it's just something that works well for me and gives me the distraction free writing experience I want. It includes a timer, some simple formating, and that's about it for now. I plan to hopefully put this on a raspberry pi zero 2 in order to boot directly into the app and use that as a simple writer deck.
